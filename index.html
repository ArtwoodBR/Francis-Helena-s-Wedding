Our Love Story

How we got here.

SOMETIMES A SHARED PAST BECOMES THE FOUNDATION OF A SHARED FUTURE…

How did we get here…?

The funny thing is… the answer depends on where you decide the story begins. If you ask us, it didn’t begin on the day we exchanged phone numbers. It began years before that. We just didn’t know it. We both attended St. Paul’s Lutheran School (SPLS) for our primary and junior high education. I was two years ahead of Helena. Our classes separated us. We were never classmates and certainly not friends, but we shared mutual friends.

Looking back now, we sometimes wonder… Maybe we stood on the same assembly grounds on Monday mornings…. Maybe we walked past each other on our way to class. Maybe, one afternoon after school had closed, we crossed paths without a second thought. Maybe during some lunch breaks, while I played football with my friends on the school compound, she was somewhere nearby with her friends, playing ampe. Just maybe…

We both have no distinct memories of ever meeting back then. Life moved on. SPLS became a cherished memory. Senior High School took us in different directions. Then came university, careers, responsibilities and adulthood. If anyone had told us that we would one day marry someone who had probably walked the same school compound as us years earlier, we would have smiled politely and dismissed it. Little did we know… God had already begun writing our story.

Then, on 31st March 2022, something happened. Two of our old SPLS schoolmates and siblings, Justice and Jasmine, sadly lost their mother. Justice is my friend. Jasmine is Helena’s friend. As friends often do in moments of grief, we all came together for the one-week observation to support them and their family. Neither of us had any idea that this gathering would become the beginning of our own story. No one came expecting anything beyond paying their respects. Certainly not us. A one-week observation is probably the last place you’d expect to notice someone. Everyone is dressed simply in black, no glamour, the atmosphere is solemn, and no one is trying to stand out. And yet… somehow… I noticed Helena. To this day, I cannot fully explain why. She was not trying to attract attention. In fact, she seemed completely unaware that anyone was watching. Perhaps it was the quiet confidence with which she carried herself. Perhaps it was the way she related with people…. not superficially, but with genuine warmth. Maybe it was the kindness in her smile despite the sadness of the occasion. Or perhaps it was the kind of beauty that first reveals itself through character before appearance. I honestly cannot tell you exactly what it was. All I knows is this - Before that day ended, I found myself mentioning her to my close friends. Before everyone parted ways, we exchanged contacts. Nothing extra… Just one more phone number of an old schoolmate. Or so we thought. That night, after the one-week observation, I sent her a simple message to make sure she had arrived home safely. That was all. No long conversations. No endless texting. No flirting. Life simply carried on.

In fact, we hardly spoke again until the day before the funeral. I reached out to find out whether she would be attending and how she planned to get there. Ever the gentleman, I even offered to pick her up, take her to the funeral and bring her safely back home afterwards. And she politely declined. The next day, we met again at the funeral. Once again, old schoolmates gathered. Once again, Helena was there. And once again…My heart reacted. Except this time, I realised that whatever I had felt at the one-week observation hadn’t disappeared.

It had quietly stayed with me. Time had not diluted it. If anything, time had confirmed it. That was the moment I stopped treating the feeling as something random. After the funeral, everything changed. We talked more. Our conversations became longer. The visits became intentional. Texts became something to look forward to. Friendship slowly made room for affection. Affection quietly became love. Love changed our names, and before long we were calling each other “Bébé.”

Now, this is probably where I should confidently tell you that I proposed. In fact, I could swear it happened! 😂 But my beautiful Helena insists that I still haven’t officially proposed. I’ve learnt that arguing with a trainee lawyer is a battle you’re unlikely to win, so I happily concede. Case closed!

I shall do the needful. 💍😄And here we are today… Hand in hand. Heart to heart. Looking back now, we smile at the irony. Who would have imagined that the little boy and little girl who unknowingly walked the same corridors of SPLS years ago would one day become two people who cannot imagine life without each other? Only God could write a story like ours.

A story that began without memories. A story that patiently waited for the right time. A story that reminds us that God’s timing is always perfect. As we celebrate our love, our prayer is that everyone reading this will never underestimate ordinary moments. Sometimes the person God has prepared for you has been closer than you think. Sometimes a season of mourning becomes the unexpected doorway to joy. And sometimes… the greatest love stories don’t begin with fireworks. They begin with a quiet, unforgettable feeling…one that simply refuses to go away.
